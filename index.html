FlyLab controls

The main playground is a fast, phone-friendly behavioral model. The 139K BRAIN button embeds the real open-source FlyBrain connectome simulator. The two layers are intentionally distinguished: the playground adds game-like interactions that the published simulator does not expose.

Playground

  • Select / drag: tap a fly to select it; drag it around. Fast dragging irritates and startles it.
  • Food / Sugar / Bitter: place stimuli. Hungry flies seek food; bitter items are avoided after tasting.
  • Poke / Scare: poke repeatedly for irritation; scare causes an escape burst and fear spike.
  • Wind: drag across the world to create a gust vector.
  • Light / Heat / Cold / Stink: alter a local patch of the environment.
  • Party: temporary flashing/sensory overload. Vacuum: pulls nearby flies toward the touch point.

Neural Lab

Possession can make the selected fly follow, flee, orbit or freeze around a target point. Conditioning pairs Cue A with food or fear and stores a per-fly association. The lesion, stimulation, drug-like, false-sensory and social controls modify only the local game-layer model; they are experiments/toys, not claims about direct control of a biological fly.

Drive overrides

The sliders are not claimed to be biological mood controls. With “Lock overrides” enabled, they clamp the selected fly’s game-layer drives for experimentation. Without the lock, the sliders simply set the current drive value and the model continues evolving.

Running locally on a phone

Opening index.html directly works for the playground on most browsers. For the embedded connectome simulator and best compatibility, serve the folder over HTTP on your local network. See README.md in the zip.
